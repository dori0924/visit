### Visitor Guidelines

> 🛡️ **Hyundai Steel is a National Key Facility of the Republic of Korea.**
> All facilities and information on site are subject to security control, so every visitor must strictly follow the security rules.

For the safety and security of our site, all visitors must follow the rules below.

#### 🔒 Collection and Use of Personal Information
- **Items collected:** Name, mobile number, nationality, purpose of visit
- **Purpose:** Visitor access control, site security, and contact in case of a safety incident
- **Retention period:** **1 year** from the date of collection (destroyed promptly afterwards)
- You may decline to provide this information, but your access to the site may be restricted.

#### 📵 No Photography
- **Taking photos, videos, or audio recordings is prohibited** anywhere on site.
- If you need to take pictures for business purposes, obtain prior approval from your host.

#### 🚷 Restricted Areas
- Do not enter areas unrelated to the purpose of your visit.
- Please **stay with your host** while moving around the site.

#### 💾 Storage Devices
- USB drives, external hard drives, and other storage devices are not allowed in security areas.
- If you must bring one in, report it to the security staff first.

#### 🚨 Emergency Evacuation
- When the alarm sounds, stop what you are doing and **follow your host's instructions** to the nearest emergency exit.
- Do not use elevators. Use the stairs.
- After evacuating, report to the designated assembly point for a headcount.
