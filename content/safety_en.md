#### 📋 Steelworks Safety Rules
1. When entering a plant in the steelworks, you must wear a **hard hat, safety shoes, leg gaiters, safety glasses, and a dust mask** as standard, plus any other protective equipment your host requests.
2. The speed limit for vehicles in the steelworks is **30 km/h or less**. Drive with your **headlights on at all times**, and stop or park only in designated places.
3. Smoking is strictly prohibited in explosion hazard areas such as hazardous material storage facilities and areas where gas can accumulate. Smoke only in **designated smoking areas**.
4. Entering marked hazardous areas and **authorized-personnel-only** areas in the steelworks without your host's permission is prohibited.
5. In an emergency, evacuate safely by following the **emergency evacuation route**.
6. Pedestrians must use the designated walkways, walk briskly without running even in an emergency, and always hold the **handrail** when using stairs.
7. If an emergency such as an injury or a fire occurs while you are on site, check the reporting location sign at that place and call the emergency number **041-680-6119**.
8. When driving across a railway crossing, you must stop first and check your surroundings before proceeding. Violations are penalized with **removal from the site for the day on the 1st violation and for 30 days on the 2nd**. (Applies to external partner companies)
