### Visitor Security and Safety Pledge

During my visit to the site, I pledge to faithfully observe the following.

1. I will not take any photos, videos, or audio recordings on site without prior approval.
2. I will not disclose to others or use without permission any confidential information, including technical, business, or management information, that I see or hear during my visit.
3. I will not enter areas unrelated to the purpose of my visit, and I will move around only as guided by my host.
4. I will not bring storage devices such as USB drives into security areas without permission, nor take any company materials off site.
5. I will follow the site's safety rules and the instructions of the staff, including the emergency evacuation procedures.
6. If I violate any of the above and cause damage to the company, I will bear civil and criminal liability under the applicable laws.
